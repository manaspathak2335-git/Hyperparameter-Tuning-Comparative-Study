# Hyperparameter-Tuning-Comparative-Study
Tech Stach: Python, Scikit-Learn, Pandas, Numpy, XGBoost, MatPlotlib, Seaborn, Google Colab.
The study utilizes PaySim Online Payment Fraud Dataset, primarily because it accurately represents the high class imbalance i.e fraudulent records typically represent a tiny fraction of the overall transactional volume, and high dimensionality associated with digital payments data. The dataset is undersampled to mitigate its high class imbalance using Random Undersampling Technique,  preserving 100% of fraud transactional records. After undersampling, data is further pre-processed to make it fit for training XGBoost algorithm, Encoding of categorical columns is performed along with Feature Engineering to create robust features and get rid of irrelevant ones. The pre-processed data is finally split into training and testing datasets, and a baseline XGBoost model is trained on training data with default hyperparameter configuration.
 In this study we optimize the XGBoost algorithm using three hyperparameter tuning techniques namely, Grid Search Optimization, Random Search Optimization and Bayesian Optimization.
 The hyperparameters taken into consideration for tuning for this study are, n_estimators (no. Of trees), learning_rate (controls the step size during each iteration), max_depth (maximum depth of each tree), subsample (fraction of sampled used to fit individual learners), cosample_bytree (fraction of features randomly samples for each tree) and scale_pos_weight (acts as a scaling multiplier applied to the loss function for the positive class, compelling the algorithm to assign higher mathematical importance to minority class instances during gradient computation). Several evaluation metrics such as, Precision, Recall, F-1 Score, Confusion Matrix, ROC-AUC & PR-AUC are used to evaluate the performance of optimized models. Models are optimized with the aim of maximization of recall, while keeping focus upon understanding and balancing the performance and computational time trade-offs.
 The final results of study conclude Bayesian Optimization to be a superior method in terms of its performance on evaluation metrics and time taken for computations. Bayesian Optimized model matched the performance of Grid Search Optimized model with Precision score of 0.727569, Recall of 0.994914, F-1 Score of 0.840494, ROC-AUC Score of 0.977730 and Average Precision of 0.980835. However it took only 285.502237 seconds to complete execution which is nearly 10 times lesser than time taken by Grid Search Optimization.
